Privacy Policy

KitchenCost for Android · Draft updated 16 October 2026

KitchenCost is an offline recipe cost calculator. This policy explains how KitchenCost and its developer handle information when you use the app or contact us.

Information you enter

KitchenCost processes the recipe information you enter to calculate costs and let you save and reopen recipes. This includes:

  • Recipe names and the number of servings.
  • Ingredient names, quantities used, package sizes and package prices.
  • Calculated ingredient costs, recipe totals and costs per serving.

Saved recipe information is stored locally in the app's storage on your device. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address or phone number to use the app.

Collection and sharing

KitchenCost does not send your recipes to the developer or provide an online recipe-sync service. We do not sell or share your recipe information with advertisers.

The app has no advertising, and its analytics and automatic crash-reporting services are disabled. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Storage and security

Recipe records are kept in the app's private storage area on your device. KitchenCost does not add separate encryption to these records. Their protection depends on your device's security and access controls.

Retention and deletion

Saved recipes remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app's stored data. The Delete action in Saved recipes removes a recipe from the active saved-recipe library. An open draft or a record retained from an older app version may remain separately.

To remove all locally stored KitchenCost records, use your Android device's settings: Apps → KitchenCost → Storage → Clear storage or Clear data. The exact labels vary by device. This removes all saved KitchenCost recipes on that device.

The developer cannot access, recover or remotely delete recipes stored on your device.

Device backups

Depending on your device and account settings, Android or your device's backup service may back up and restore app data. These backups are controlled by your backup provider. Clearing app data does not necessarily remove existing backups; manage those separately through your device or backup account settings. KitchenCost does not operate a cloud backup service.

Contacting the developer

If you email us, we receive your email address and any message or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and resolve the issue. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store it.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the enquiry, unless a longer period is required by law. You can request deletion of correspondence by contacting us at the address below.

This policy website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it connects your browser to GitHub, which may process technical request information under its privacy statement. This page contains no advertising or embedded analytics scripts.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data handling changes. The updated policy will be published here with a revised date.
